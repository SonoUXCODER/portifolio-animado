Brand Site / Lead Routing / 2025

A Rio de Janeiro practice was receiving family, labour and social-security cases through the same phone number, with no context attached. The idea was to turn the first conversation inside out: the triage happens before the contact, not during it.

The challenge

What wasbroken.

Everything arrived through one channel with no context. The lawyer spent the first half hour of every conversation working out what the case was even about, and a good share of those cases were not hers to take.

The approach

How itwas made.

  1. Research

    Sorting a year of first messages

    I grouped the incoming enquiries by what the person actually needed, not by how they phrased it. Eight clusters came out, and those eight clusters became the architecture of the site.

  2. UX Strategy

    Triage before contact

    Each practice area is its own page with its own language, so people self-select before writing anything. The short form at the end of each page composes a message that already says which area it belongs to.

  3. Wireframes

    One answer per screen

    Legal copy is dense by nature, so each screen carries one idea and one exit. The route from “I have this problem” to “message written” is four screens with no dead ends.

  4. UI Design

    Serious without being cold

    Bone paper, near-black text, a single muted rose for emphasis. The portrait is large and warm on purpose: for this kind of decision people are choosing a person, not a firm.

  5. Development

    Eight static pages, indexed properly

    Hand-written HTML and CSS, structured data for the practice, one script for the form. It loads in under a second on mobile data, which is where most of the traffic comes from.
